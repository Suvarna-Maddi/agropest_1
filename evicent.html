Evicent

Major Crops: Maize, Mirchi, Cauliflower...

Mode of Action: Ensures effective kill of the target pest with a dual mode of action. The pest stops feeding within 1–2 hours and dies within 12–24 hours. It acts on GABA receptors, inhibiting muscle contraction, and also disrupts the formation of endo-cuticle, resulting in fast action and long-lasting control.

Target Pests: Effective against all life stages of insect pests, from eggs to adults.

Presentation Available: 5 g, 24 g, 60 g, 120 g

Company: Syngenta
