Phantom

Technical Name: NOVALURON 5.25% + INDOXACARB 4.5% SC (PHANTOM)

Mode of Action: Inhibits chitin synthesis, disrupting insect molting, and blocks sodium ion channels in the nervous system, leading to paralysis and death.

Major Crops: Rice, Pulses, Vegetables

Target Pest: Black Gram Pod Borer Complex, Fruit Borer, Fruit Borer Complex, Gram Pod Borer, Leaf Eating Caterpillar, Pod Borer, Rice Leaf Folder

Presentation Available: 100ml, 250ml, 500ml, 1l
