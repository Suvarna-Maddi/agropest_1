Pedestal

Works against the larvae of insects of the orders Lepidoptera, Coleoptera, Homoptera and Diptera

Technical Name: Novaluron 10% EC

Major Crops: Cotton, Cabbage, Chilli, Tomato, Bengal Gram

Mode of action: Pedestal active against larvae of insects of the orders Lepidoptera, Coleoptera, Homoptera and Diptera. It acts by inhibiting chitin formation during moulting.

Target Pest: American bollworm, Diamond back moth, Fruit borer, Tobacco caterpillar, Pod borer

Presentation Available: 50ml, 100ml, 250ml, 500ml and 1ltr.

Company: Adama
