Sypro-C

Technical Name: PROFENOFOS 40% + CYPERMETHRIN 4% EC

Mode of Action: Synergistic action of Profenofos and Cypermethrin with strong translaminar and ovicidal properties. Controls sucking pests as well as bollworms.

Major Crops: Cotton, Chillies, Tur, etc.

Target Pest: Thrips, Bollworm, Aphids, Jassids, Mealybug, etc.

Dose/Acre: 500 ml to 600 ml per acre

Presentation Available: 100 ml, 250 ml, 500 ml, 1 L

Company: Prism
