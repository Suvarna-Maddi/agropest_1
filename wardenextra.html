warden Extra

Technical Name: Trifloxystrobin 6% + Thiamethoxam 24% + Thiophanate Methyl 9.5% FS

Major Crops: Okra Crops

Mode of action:

Trifloxystrobin: Inhibits fungal respiration, disrupting energy production.

Thiamethoxam: Acts as a systemic insecticide, affecting the nervous system of insects.

Thiophanate Methyl: Inhibits fungal cell division by disrupting microtubule formation.

Target pests: Damping off (Rhizoctonia solani) and Leafhopper (Amrasca biguttula biguttulai), Aphid (Aphis gossypii), Whitefly (Bemisia tabaci)

Presentation Available: 1 Ltr, 500 ml, 200 ml, 100 ml, 50 ml

Company: Best Agros
