Tapuz

Technical Name: Buprofezin 15% + Acephate 35% w/w WP

Major Crops: Paddy

Mode of action: It acts as an insect growth regulator (inhibits moulting) and also simultaneously affects the nervous system of the insect by inhibiting the transmission of messages through disruption of acetyl choline (leads to paralysis and death).

Target Pest: Brown plant hopper, White backed plant hopper, Jassids, Thrips, Aphids, Whiteflies

Presentation Available: 100g, 250g, 500g, 1kg

Company: Adama
