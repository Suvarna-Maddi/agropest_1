Vistara

Technical Name: Chlorantraniliprole 0.4% w/w GR

Mode of Action: Vistara is a systemic plant insecticide belonging to the anthranilic diamide group. It acts as a Ryanodine Receptor Activator, disrupting normal muscle functions in pests. Activation leads to unregulated release of Ca²⁺ from the sarcoplasmic reticulum in muscle cells, causing impaired muscle function, feeding cessation, lethargy, and eventual insect death. The formulation exhibits excellent ovicidal and larvicidal activity through ingestion.

Major Crops: Sugarcane, Rice

Target Pest: High larvicidal potency pests; effective through ingestion

Dose: 4–7.5 kg per acre

Presentation Available: 4 kg, 1 kg

Company: Best Agros
