Imperial

An effective non-systemic insecticide & acaricide

Technical Name: Bifenthrin 10% w/w EC

Major Crops: Rice, Cotton, Sugarcane

Mode of action: Imperial affects insects by stomach and contact action. Imperial acts on the nervous system of insects and disturbs the function of neurons by interaction with the sodium channel.

Target Pest: Bollworm & Whitefly, Stem Border, Leaf folder, Green leaf hoppers, Termites

Presentation Available: 100ml, 250ml, 500ml, 1ltr and 5ltrs.

Company: Adama
