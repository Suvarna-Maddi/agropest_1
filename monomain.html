Monomain

Broad spectrum of pests in a wide range of crops

Technical Name: Organophosphate

Major Crops: Rice, Maize, Black gram, Green gram, Red gram

Mode of action: Monomain is a systemic insecticide and acaricide with contact and stomach action. The product controls a broad spectrum of pests and in a wide range of crops.

Target Pest: Brown plant hopper, Yellow stem border, Green leaf hopper, Leaf roller/folder, Shoot fly, Pod borer, Plume mouth, Pod fly

Presentation Available: 100ml, 250ml, 500ml, 1ltr and 5ltrs.

Company: Adama
