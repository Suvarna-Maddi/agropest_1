Lamdex Super

For the effective control of insects and pests on a wide variety of crops

Technical Name: Lambdacyhalothrin 5% EC

Major Crops: Rice, Cotton, Vegetables, Fruits

Mode of action: Lamdex Super is a non systemic, photostable third generation pyrethroid insecticide with contact and stomach action and repellent properties giving rapid knockdown and long residual activity.

Target Pest: Bollworm, Jassids, Thrips, Leaf folder, Stem borer, Gall midge, Hispa, Green leaf hopper, Fruit borer, Pod borer, Mite, Pod fly, Hoppers...

Presentation Available: 100ml, 250ml, 500ml, 1ltr and 5ltrs.
