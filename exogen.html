Exogen

Technical Name: Exogen

Mode of Action: Plant-based pesticide formulated with alkaloids and proteins that protect crops from sucking insects. Acts by forming an emulsion that is sprayed on crops for effective pest control.

Major Crops: All Crops

Target Use: Controls sucking insect pests using natural alkaloids (from quinine, cola nut, cinchona bark) and proteins (from egg albumen or milk casein). Safe and effective for sustainable agriculture.

Presentation Available: 500ml, 250ml, 100ml
