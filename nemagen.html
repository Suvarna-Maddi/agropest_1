Nemagen

Technical Name: Chlorantraniliprole 4.5% + Novaluron 11.5% + Emamectin Benzoate 1.5% SE

Mode of Action: A combination insecticide effective for the control of Shoot & Fruit Borer on Brinjal. It works through contact and ingestion, disrupting the pest's muscle function and development.

Major Crops: Brinjal

Target Pest: Shoot & Fruit Borer

Presentation Available: 1 L, 500 ml, 250 ml, 100 ml

Company: Best Agros
