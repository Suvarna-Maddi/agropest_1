Constop Plus

Technical Name: Hexaconazole 5% SC

Major Crops: Cereals, Vegetables, Fruits, Oilseeds, Legumes

Mode of action: Inhibits ergosterol synthesis by targeting the enzyme lanosterol demethylase, disrupting fungal cell membranes

Target Pest/Disease: Effective against a variety of fungal diseases such as leaf spots, blights, and powdery mildew

Presentation Available: 250ml, 500ml, 1ltr

Company: Preventis
