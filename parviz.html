Parviz

A unique solution for the control of sucking pests

Technical Name: Fipronil 4% + Acetamiprid 4% w/w SC

Major Crops: Cotton

Mode of action: Parviz has a dual mode of action. It affects both the Central Nervous System & the Peripheral Nervous System simultaneously, which no other chemical exhibits. Thus, Parviz has double impact on the insect Nervous System, resulting in immediate death of the insect.

Target Pest: Jassids, Aphids, Whiteflies

Presentation Available: 100ml, 250ml, 500ml and 1ltr.

Company: Adama
