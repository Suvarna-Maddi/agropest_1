Nelsi

Technical Name: Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% W/W SC (NELSA)

Mode of Action: Acts on insect ryanodine receptors, causing uncontrolled muscle contraction, paralysis, and death. Most effective via ingestion of treated plant material, providing long-lasting protection against pests.

Major Crops: All Crops

Target Pest: Stem Borer, Termite, American Bollworm, Fruit Borer, Pod Borer, etc.

Presentation Available: 10ml, 30ml, 60ml, 150ml

Company: Ayushi
