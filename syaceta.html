Syaceta

Technical Name: Acetamiprid 20% SP

Major Crops: Cotton, Rice, Vegetables, Fruit Trees

Mode of Action / Use: Syaceta is a systemic insecticide with translaminar activity. It acts as an agonist in the insect nervous system, exhibiting triple action: ovicidal, larvicidal, and adulticidal. Its unique translaminar property protects both sides of the leaf for long-lasting control.

Target Pest: Bollworms, Jassids, Thrips, Fruit borer, Aphids, Whitefly, Leafhoppers, Plant bugs

Dose / Acre: 30 gm to 80 gm per acre

Presentation Available: 50 gm, 100 gm, 250 gm, 500 gm, 1 kg

Company: Prism
