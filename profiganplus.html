Profigan Plus

Technical Name: Profenophos 40% EC + Cypermethrin 4% EC

Major Crops: Cotton

Mode of action: Profigan Plus is a combination of Profenofos, an organophosphate, and Cypermethrin, a pyrethroid with contact and stomach action.

Target Pest: Bollworm complex

Presentation Available: 100ml, 250ml, 500ml, 1ltr and 5ltrs

Company: Adama
