Agro Star

Technical Name: AGROSTAR

Mode of Action: Organic fertilizer prepared through anaerobic and aerobic decomposition of agricultural residues. Enriched with beneficial microorganisms that enhance nutrient absorption from the soil.

Major Crops: All Crops

Target Use: Provides 3 crore beneficial microorganisms per 100g, improving soil health and nutrient uptake.

Dosage: 1–2 gm per liter of water
