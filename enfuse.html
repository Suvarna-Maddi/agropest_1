Enfuse

For effective control of lepidopteran insect pests in rice & sugarcane

Technical Name: Chlorantrantraniliprole 0.4% w/w GR

Major Crops: Rice, Sugarcane

Mode of action: Enfuse makes the stored calcium in the muscle cells deplete by activating the ryanodine receptors to release calcium. This action causes impairment of the muscular function leading to paralysis and death of the insect.

Target Pest: Yellow stem borer, Leaf folder, Early shoot borer, Top borer

Presentation Available: 4kg

Company: Adama
