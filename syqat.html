Syqat

Technical Name: Captan 70% + Hexaconazole 5% WP

Major Crops: Fruits, Vegetables, Various other crops

Mode of Action:

Captan: Acts as a protective fungicide by preventing spore germination and fungal growth through contact action.

Hexaconazole: Inhibits ergosterol synthesis, disrupting fungal cell membranes and preventing fungal growth.

Target Pest: Powdery Mildew, Anthracnose, Late Blight, Early Blight, Downy Mildew, Grey Mildew

Presentation Available: 250gm, 500gm

Company: Prism
