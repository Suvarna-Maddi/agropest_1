Harrier

An effective insecticide from control of sucking pests

Technical Name: Acetamiprid 20% SP

Major Crops: Rice, Cotton, Cabbage, Chilli, Okra.

Mode of action: Harrier is a systemic insecticide with translaminar activity and with contact and stomach action.

Target Pest: Whiteflies, Aphid, Thrips, Jassid, Brown plant hoppers.

Presentation Available: 20g, 50g, 100g, 250g, 500g.

Company: Adama
