Boregan G

Effective Control for Leaf folder and Stem borer in Rice

Technical Name: Cartap Hydrochloride 4% GR

Major Crops: Rice

Mode of action: It is a systemic insecticide with stomach and contact action. The treated insects discontinue feeding, and die of starvation.

Target Pest: Stem border, Leaf folder, Whorl Maggot

Company: Adama
