Minect Xtra

Technical Name: Cyantraniliprole 16.9% + Lufenuron 16.9% SC (MINECTO XTRA)

Mode of Action: Combines the activity of Cyantraniliprole and Lufenuron, increasing insecticidal activity against Lepidopteran pests. Exhibits ovicidal, transovarial, and translaminar activity, effective against all life stages of Lepidopteran pests. Adjuvants may improve control of other pests.

Major Crops: Rice

Target Pest: Lepidopteran pests (all life stages)

Presentation Available: 10ml, 20ml, 50ml, 100ml

Company: Syngenta
