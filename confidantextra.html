Confidant Extra

Technical Name: Imidacloprid 70% WG

Mode of Action: CONFIDANT EXTRA acts through systemic action (absorbed by the plant and affecting insects feeding on it), contact action (killing pests on direct contact), and neurotoxic effect (blocking nicotinic acetylcholine receptors in insects, causing paralysis and death). This ensures quick knockdown and effective control of sucking pests.

Major Crops: Cotton, Paddy, Vegetables, Fruit crops, Sugarcane, Tea plantations, Cereals, Oilseeds

Target Pest: Aphids, Whiteflies, Jassids, Termites, BPH, GLH, WBPH, Thrips

Presentation Available: 75 g, 150 g, 300 g
