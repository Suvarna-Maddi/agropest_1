Symida Top

Technical Name: Imidacloprid 30.5% SC

Major Crops: Cotton, Ladyfinger, Groundnut

Mode of Action / Use: Symida Top is a neonicotinoid insecticide with systemic action. It can be applied as a foliar spray, soil drench, seedling dip, or seed treatment. It is effective against termites and various sucking pests.

Target Pest: Jassid, Aphid, Thrips, Termite, Mites

Presentation Available: 100 ml, 250 ml, 500 ml, 1 L

Company: Prism
