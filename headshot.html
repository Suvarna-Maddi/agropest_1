Headshot

Technical Name: Halosulfuron Methyl 75% WG

Mode of action: Headshot (Halosulfuron Methyl 75% WG) is a post-emergence broad-spectrum herbicide belonging to the sulfonylurea group. It has excellent foliar application efficacy against Cyperus rotundus in sugarcane, and Cyperus rotundus & Cyperus iria in maize and bottle gourd crops.

Major Crops: Sugarcane, Maize, Bottle gourd

Target Pest: Grassy weeds, Broadleaf weeds

Presentation Available: 36g, 100g

Company: Best Agros
