Paranex

A Pre Emergence Herbicide

Technical Name: Paraquat Dichloride 24% SL

Major Crops: Tea, Potato, Cotton, Rubber

Mode of action: Paraquat is a fast-acting, non-selective contact herbicide. It interferes with photosynthesis by diverting electrons from photosystem I, leading to the formation of highly reactive free radicals that damage cell membranes, ultimately causing rapid desiccation and death of weeds.

Target Pest: Broadleaf Weeds, Monocot Weeds

Presentation Available: 250ml, 500ml, 1ltr, 5ltrs

Company: Adama
