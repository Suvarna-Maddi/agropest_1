Cliff

Technical Name: Carbendazim 12% + Mancozeb 63%

Major Crops: Cereals, Vegetables, Fruits, Legumes, Oilseeds

Mode of action:

Carbendazim: A systemic fungicide that inhibits fungal cell division by interfering with microtubule formation, disrupting mitosis during cell division.

Mancozeb: A protective fungicide that prevents fungal spore germination and growth by interfering with various fungal enzymes.

Target Pest/Disease: Leaf spots, Blights, Rusts, Downy Mildew, Powdery Mildew, Anthracnose

Presentation Available: 500g, 1kg

Company: Preventis
