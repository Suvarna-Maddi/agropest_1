KavachFlo

Technical Name: Chlorothalonil 40% + Difenoconazole 4%

Major Crops: Tomato, Chilli

Mode of Action:

Chlorothalonil: Acts as a broad-spectrum protective fungicide by inhibiting multiple fungal enzymes and interfering with spore germination.

Difenoconazole: Inhibits ergosterol synthesis, disrupting fungal cell membrane formation.

Target Pest: Early blight, Late blight, Leaf spots, Anthracnose, Fruit rot

Dose: 400 ml / acre

Presentation Available: 200 ml, 400 ml

Company: Syngenta
