Ayustara-75

Technical Name: THIAMETHOXAM 75% SG (Ayustara-75)

Mode of Action: Acts by disrupting the nervous system of insects through nicotinic acetylcholine receptor (nAChR) agonism, causing paralysis and death. Being systemic, it is absorbed by the plant and provides long-lasting protection against sucking and chewing pests.

Major Crops: Rice, Cotton, Sugarcane, Groundnut

Target Pest: Termites, Early Shoot Borer, Green Leaf Hopper, Brown Plant Hopper, Jassids, Thrips

Presentation Available: 5gm, 125gm, 250gm

Company: Ayushi
