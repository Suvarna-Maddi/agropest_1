Technical Name: CARTAP HYDROCHLORIDE 50% S.P

Mode of Action: Neurotoxic insecticide that disrupts the normal function of nerve cells in insects, affecting the central nervous system and leading to paralysis and death.

Target Pest: Stem Borers, Leaf Folders, Leaf Miners, Fruit Borers, Diamondback Moth, Caterpillars, Cutworms

Presentation Available: 50g, 100g, 250g, 500g, 1kg
