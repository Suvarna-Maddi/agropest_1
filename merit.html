Merit

Technical Name: Imidacloprid 70% WG

Mode of Action: Imidacloprid is a neonicotinoid insecticide that interferes with nerve impulse transmission in insects. It mimics the action of acetylcholine, causing overstimulation and blockage of nerve receptors, leading to paralysis and death.

Major Crops: Fruits, Vegetables (depending on regional approvals and label instructions), Turfgrass, Ornamental plants (trees, shrubs, flowers)

Target Pest: White Grubs, European Crane Fly Larvae, Japanese Beetles, Aphids, Whiteflies, Leafhoppers, Thrips, Plant Bugs, Scale Insects

Presentation Available: 100 ml, 1 L, 150 g

Company: Preventis
