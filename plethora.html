Plethora

Broad spectrum Lepidopteran insecticide

Technical Name: Novaluron 5.25% + Indoxacarb 4.5% w/w SC

Major Crops: Black gram, Chickpea, Groundnut, Pigeon pea, Redgram (Tur), Soybean, Tomato, Chilli, Rice.

Mode of action: Plethora acts as a chitin synthesis inhibitor and also affects the insect nervous system by inhibiting sodium ions entry into nerve cells. Hence, the insecticide hampers moulting & also paralyses the insect.

Target Pest: Black gram pod borer complex, Fruit borer, Fruit borer complex, Gram pod borer, Leaf eating caterpillar, Pod borer, Rice leaf folder.

Presentation Available: 50ml, 100ml, 350ml, 500ml, 1ltr.

Company: Adama
