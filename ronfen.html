Ronfen

Technical Name: Pyriproxyfen 8% + Dinotefuron 5% + Diafenthiuron 18% SC

Mode of Action: Dual-action insecticide with systemic and contact activity, providing effective control of sucking pests.

Target Pest: Whitefly, Jassids, Aphids, Thrips

Presentation Available: 1 L, 500 ml, 250 ml, 100 ml, 50 ml
