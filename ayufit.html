Ayufit

Technical Name: Pretilachlor 50% EC

Major Crops: Cotton

Use/Benefits: Ayufit is a broad-spectrum, selective pre-emergence to early post-emergence herbicide for rice. It exhibits highly efficient control of a variety of weeds, including annual grasses, sedges, and broadleaf weeds, by inhibiting growth and reducing cell division.

Target Pest: Echinochloa crusgalli, Echinochloa colonum, Cyperus difformis, Cyperus iria, Fimbristylis miliacea, Eclipta alba, Ludwigia pulviflora, Leptochloa chinensis, Monochoria vaginalis, Panicum repens

Presentation Available: 250ml, 500ml, 1ltr

Company: Ayushi
