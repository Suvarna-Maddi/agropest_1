Agadi Super

Broad spectrum insecticide

Technical Name: phenylpyrazole group

Major Crops: Rice, Sugarcane, Cotton, Maize, Soybean

Mode of action: It is a broad spectrum insecticide with contact and ingestion mode of action.

Target Pest: Stem borers, Brown planthoppers, Whiteflies, Thrips, Rootworms, Termites

Presentation Available: 40g, 100g, 500g, 1kg.
