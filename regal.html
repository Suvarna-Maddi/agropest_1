Regal

Technical Name: Ethion 40% + Cypermethrin 5% EC

Mode of Action: Ethion: Organophosphate insecticide that inhibits acetylcholinesterase, causing accumulation of acetylcholine in the nervous system, leading to paralysis and death. Cypermethrin: Pyrethroid insecticide that disrupts voltage-gated sodium channels, causing prolonged nerve signal transmission, paralysis, and death.

Major Crops: Cotton, Maize, Soybeans, Peppers, Beans, Grapes, Citrus

Target Pest: Beetles, Aphids, Caterpillars, Thrips, Whiteflies, Moths

Dose: 625–1000 ml of formulation in 500–1000 Liters of water

Presentation Available: 250ml, 500ml

Company: Preventis
