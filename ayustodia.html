Ayustodia

Technical Name: AYUSTODIA (Azoxystrobin 11% + Tebuconazole 18.3% SC)

Mode of action: Combines triazole and strobilurin chemistries to inhibit fungal respiration and ergosterol synthesis. Exhibits systemic and translaminar movement, ensuring rapid dispersion within the plant for effective protection.

Target Pest/Disease: Anthracnose, Powdery Mildew, Downy Mildew, Rust

Presentation Available: 250ml, 500ml, 1ltr
