Symoxone

With Propaquizafop 10% Agil selectively controls grassy weeds in a wide range of broad-leaf crops like Soybean, Onion, Black gram, Chickpea, and more!

Technical Name: Paraquat Dichloride 24% SL

Major Crops: Cotton, Heliotrope, Soybean, Haricot bean

Use/Benefits: Symoxone is a non-selective herbicide widely used to control weeds in gardens, field crops, vegetables, and plantations. It can kill ruderal weeds without tillage and is also used in grassveld renewal and chemical weed eradication on non-crop land.

Target Pest: Annual and perennial broadleaf weeds, annual and perennial grasses, some sedges

Presentation Available: 250ml, 500ml, 1ltr
