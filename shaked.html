Dual Action Herbicide for Broad Leaves & Grassy weeds

Technical Name: Propaquizafop 2.5% + Imazethapyr 3.75% w/w ME

Major Crops: Soybean, Groundnut, Peanuts, Chickpeas

Mode of action:

Propaquizafop: Inhibits acetyl-CoA carboxylase (ACCase) in grasses, disrupting lipid biosynthesis which leads to the death of grass weeds.

Imazethapyr: Inhibits the enzyme acetolactate synthase (ALS), affecting the biosynthesis of essential amino acids and leading to the death of broadleaf weeds and some grasses.

Target Pest: Broadleaf Weeds, Monocot Weeds

Presentation Available: 800ml
