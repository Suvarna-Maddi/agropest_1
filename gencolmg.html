Gencol-Mg

Technical Name: Gencol-Mg (Formula No. 7)

Mode of Action: Mineral mixture containing zinc, manganese, and iron in soluble form for easy absorption by crop roots. Acts as an organic fertilizer without harmful chemicals or synthetic additives.

Major Crops: All Crops

Target Use: Prevents mineral deficiencies, restores soil fertility, improves crop yield, and enhances quality of produce.

Presentation Available: 10kg, 1kg
