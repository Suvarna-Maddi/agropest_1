Eyal

Technical Name: Penoxsulam 1.02% + Cyhalofop-Butyl 5.1% OD

Major Crops: Rice

Mode of action:

Penoxsulam: Inhibits the enzyme acetolactate synthase (ALS), which is essential for amino acid biosynthesis in plants. It controls a range of broadleaf weeds and some grasses.

Cyhalofop-Butyl: Acts as an acetyl-CoA carboxylase (ACCase) inhibitor, targeting grasses and some sedges. It prevents the synthesis of fatty acids needed for cell membrane formation.

Target Pest: Broadleaf Weeds, Grass Weeds

Presentation Available: 250ml, 500ml, 1ltr

Company: Adama
