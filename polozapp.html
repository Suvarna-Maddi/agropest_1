Polo-Zapp

Technical Name: 1-[(6-chloro-3-pyridyl)methyl]-N-nitro-1H-imidazole-3-amine (Thiamethoxam)

Mode of Action: Neonicotinoid insecticide that mimics acetylcholine, overstimulating the nervous system of insects, causing paralysis and death.

Major Crops: Fruits, Vegetables, Cotton, Rice, Soybeans

Target Pest: Aphids, Whiteflies, Thrips, Leafhoppers, Plant Bugs, Termites

Presentation Available: 100ml, 250ml, 500ml, 1ltr

Company: Syngenta
