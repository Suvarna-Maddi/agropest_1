Plesiva

Technical Name: Imidacloprid (Plesiva)

Mode of Action: Systemic insecticide that inhibits neurotransmission in insects, affecting the central nervous system and causing paralysis and death.

Major Crops: Rice, Cotton, Soybeans, Maize, Vegetables, Fruits

Target Pest: Aphids, Whiteflies, Leafhoppers, Thrips, Termites

Presentation Available: 125ml, 250ml, 625ml

Company: Syngenta
