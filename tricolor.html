Tricolor

Technical Name: Trifloxystrobin 10% + Difenoconazole 12.5% + Sulfur 3% SC

Major Crops: Tomato

Mode of Action:

Trifloxystrobin: Inhibits fungal respiration, disrupting energy production.

Difenoconazole: Inhibits ergosterol synthesis, disrupting fungal cell membranes.

Sulfur: Acts as a protective fungicide, inhibiting fungal spore germination and enzyme activity.

Target Pest: Early Blight & Late Blight

Presentation Available: 250ml, 500ml, 1 Liter

Company: Best Agros
