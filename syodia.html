Syodia

Technical Name: Azoxystrobin 11% + Tebuconazole 18.3% SC

Major Crops: Cereals, Vegetables, Fruits, Ornamentals

Mode of Action:

Azoxystrobin: Inhibits respiration in fungi, disrupting energy production.

Tebuconazole: Inhibits ergosterol synthesis, disrupting fungal cell membranes.

Target Pest: Powdery Mildew, Rusts, Leaf Spots, Downy Mildew, Blights

Dose: 600–750 ml of formulation in 500–800 liters of water

Presentation Available: 100 ml, 250 ml, 500 ml, 1 Liter

Company: Prism
