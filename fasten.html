Technical Name: Fipronil 5% SC

Mode of Action: Fipronil is toxic to insects by contact or ingestion. Fipronil blocks GABAA-gated chloride channels in the central nervous system.

Major Crops: Cotton, Sugarcane, Rice, Cabbage & Chilli crop.

Target Pest: Aphids, Jassids, Thrips, Whitefly, Boll Worm, Root borer, Early shoot borer, Stem borer, Brown plant hopper, Green leaf hopper, Leaf folder, Rice gall midge, Whorl maggot, White backed plant hopper, Diamond back moth, Thrips, Aphid & Fruit borer.

Dose/Acre: 400-600 ml per acre

Presentation Available: 1Ltr, 500ml & 250ml
