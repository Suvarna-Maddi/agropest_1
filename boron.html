Boron

Technical Name: BORON

Mode of Action: Vital micronutrient fertilizer that strengthens cell walls, enhances nutrient transport, and improves reproductive processes in plants.

Major Crops: All Crops

Target Use: Corrects boron and magnesium deficiencies. Each 1 kg provides Boron along with Magnesium Sulphate (Mg 9.6%), supporting plant health and optimizing crop productivity.

Dosage: Mix 2.5 g per liter of water and spray on leaves 30 days after germination or pruning.
