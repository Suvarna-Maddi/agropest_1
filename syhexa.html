Syhexa

Technical Name: Hexaconazole 5% EC

Major Crops: Paddy (Rice), Grapes, Chillies, Vegetables, Fruit crops, Cereals

Mode of Action:

Protectant: Provides a barrier to prevent fungal infection.

Eradicant: Controls existing infections by eliminating fungal pathogens.

Systemic: Moves within the plant to provide internal protection and control.

Target Pest: Sheath Blight, Leaf Spots, Blights, Powdery Mildew

Presentation Available: 250 ml, 500 ml, 1 L

Company: Prism
