Pegasus

Technical Name: 3-mesityl-2-oxo-1-oxaspiro[4.4]non-3-en-4-yl 3,3-dimethylbutanoate

Mode of Action: Disrupts neurotransmission in insects, leading to paralysis and death. May be systemic, affecting pests that feed on treated plants.

Major Crops: Fruits, Vegetables, Ornamental Plants

Target Pest: Aphids, Whiteflies, Thrips, and other sucking insects

Presentation Available: 100ml, 250ml, 500ml, 1ltr

Company: Syngenta
