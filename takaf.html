Takaf

Technical Name: Diafenthiuron 47% + Bifenthrin 9.4% SC

Major Crops: Cotton, Chilli

Mode of action: Takaf inhibits mitochondrial ATP synthase leading to paralysis of the pest and simultaneously affects the central and peripheral nervous system by interfering with sodium channel gating.

Target Pest: Aphids, Leaf hopper, Thrips, White fly

Presentation Available: 250ml, 500ml, 1ltr

Company: Adama
