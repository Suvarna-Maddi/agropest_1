Natgen

Technical Name: Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% w/w SC

Mode of Action: Member of the anthranilic diamide class of insecticides. Targets ryanodine receptors in insect muscles, disrupting calcium ion regulation, causing continuous muscle contraction and paralysis.

Major Crops: Rice, Cotton, Corn (Maize), Soybean, Potatoes, Tomatoes, Peppers, Cucumbers, Apples, Grapes, Citrus Fruits

Target Pest: Caterpillars (Bollworms, Corn Borers, Armyworms), Leafminers, Weevils, Fruit Flies

Presentation Available: 60ml

Company: Natco
