Bigboss

Technical Name: Lambda Cyhalothrin 5% EC

Mode of Action: A synthetic pyrethroid insecticide that acts on the nervous system of insects. It disrupts the normal function of voltage-gated sodium channels, leading to prolonged nerve signal transmission, paralysis, and ultimately death of the pest.

Major Crops: Maize, Cotton, Rice, Vegetables, Fruits, etc.

Target Pest: Aphids, Whiteflies, Leafhoppers, Thrips, Plant Bugs, and various sucking pests.

Presentation Available: 100ml, 250ml, 500ml, 1ltr

Company: Preventis
