Novoxa

Technical Name: Novaluron 5.25% + Indoxacarb 4.5% SC

Mode of Action: Broad-spectrum lepidopteran insecticide effective against multiple caterpillar pests through contact and ingestion.

Major Crops: Tomato, Rice, Black Gram, Chilli, Soybean, Red Gram, Chickpea, Groundnut

Target Pest: Fruit Borer, Leaf Borer, Pod Borer, Leaf-Eating Caterpillar

Presentation Available: 1 L, 500 ml, 250 ml, 100 ml

Company: Best Agros
