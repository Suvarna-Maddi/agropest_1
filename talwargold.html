Talwar Gold

Technical Name: Talwar Gold

Mode of Action: Biological pesticide that enhances plant resistance, enabling plants to produce natural compounds to protect themselves from pests like Maruca caterpillar, bud worm, Prodenia, Heliothis, and stem borers.

Major Crops: All Crops

Target Use: Provides sustainable pest control, reducing the need for chemical pesticides while protecting plant health and supporting environmentally friendly farming practices.

Presentation Available: 100ml, 50ml
