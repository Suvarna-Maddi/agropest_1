Letham

Technical Name: Emamectin Benzoate 1.9% EC

Mode of Action: LETHAM works by contact and ingestion, killing pests when they come into contact with or consume treated plants. It has ovicidal and larvicidal action, destroying larvae immediately after hatching. The formulation is rainfast within 2 hours, ensuring effectiveness even after rainfall.

Major Crops: Cotton, Rice, Vegetables, Pulses

Target Pest: Bollworms, Fruit Borers, Leaf Folders, Hispa, Green Semi-looper, Pod Borers, Griddle Beetle, Tobacco Caterpillar

Presentation Available: 250 ml, 500 ml, 1 L

Company: Ayushi
