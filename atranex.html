Atranex

Technical Name: Atrazine 50% WP

Major Crops: Maize, Sugarcane

Mode of action: Atranex is an herbicide of the triazine group used for pre-emergence and post-emergence control of a wide spectrum of annual grass and broadleaf weeds.

Target Pest: Trianthama, Monogyna, Digera arvensis, Echinochloa spp., Eleusine spp., Cleome viscose, Tribulus terristris

Presentation Available: 500gm, 1kg

Company: Adama
