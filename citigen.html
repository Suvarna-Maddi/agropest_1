Citigen

Technical Name: Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% w/w SC

Mode of Action: Citigen is a systemic insecticide from the anthranilic diamide group. It acts as a Ryanodine Receptor Activator, disrupting normal muscle function in pests. This causes unregulated release of calcium from muscle cells, leading to paralysis, feeding lethargy, and ultimately insect death.

Major Crops: Cotton, Rice, Brinjal, Black Gram, Bittergourd, etc.

Dose/Acre: 60–150 ml per acre

Presentation Available: 300 ml, 150 ml, 60 ml, 10 ml

Company: Best Agros
